THE VOICE — the simplest way to put this online
===============================================

This folder is the whole site. index.html contains everything: the page,
the styles and all the code. There is nothing to install and nothing to
build.

TO PUT IT ONLINE
----------------
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag THIS FOLDER onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address. That is your live site.

That is the whole deployment. Nothing else is required to see it working.

FIRST TIME YOU OPEN YOUR SITE
-----------------------------
The site asks you to sign in, and there is a large CONNECT SUPABASE button
on that screen. Accounts need a free Supabase project behind them — the
site cannot store logins on its own.

  1. Sign up free at https://supabase.com and create a project.
  2. In Supabase: Project Settings -> API.
  3. Copy the "Project URL" and the "anon public" key.
  4. Back on YOUR site (the Netlify address from above), click
     CONNECT SUPABASE and paste both in.

Once connected, that button becomes a quiet "Change Supabase project" link
and the sign-in form appears. Create your account and you are in.

The anon key is meant to be public — Supabase enforces access on its own
servers. It is not a secret, and pasting it here is how it is designed to
work.

TAKING PAYMENTS (optional)
--------------------------
Only needed if you want to charge for plans. The full walkthrough is in
supabase/README.md in the source zip: create the database table, deploy the
Stripe webhook, then paste your Stripe payment links into Settings on your
site.

WHAT IF THE PAGE LOOKS EMPTY?
-----------------------------
Give it a few seconds on first load — the neural voices are fetched on
demand. If a message appears saying the scripts did not load, the file was
probably uploaded incompletely; drag the folder across again.
